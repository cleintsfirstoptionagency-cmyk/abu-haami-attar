## 📦 2. Dual Logistics Setup: Delhivery + Shiprocket

### Can both run together without conflict?
**YES, 100%.** You do not need to choose only one.

### How it works:
1. **Shiprocket App** (Shopify App Store):
   - Aggregator with 15+ couriers (Delhivery, BlueDart, DTDC, Xpressbees, Shadowfax).
   - Displays real-time cost comparison per pincode (e.g. DTDC ₹38 vs Delhivery ₹44 vs BlueDart ₹59).
2. **Delhivery One App** (Shopify App Store):
   - Direct integration with Delhivery's enterprise network.
   - Ideal when your order volume crosses 500+ shipments/month, allowing direct negotiated corporate rates.

### Operational Strategy for Max Profit:
- When an order arrives in Shopify, it appears simultaneously on **both** dashboards.
- For metro / high-speed priority orders: Select BlueDart Air via Shiprocket.
- For bulk / remote tiers: Compare rates and click **Ship** on the cheapest courier.
- The AWB tracking code automatically pushes back to Shopify, which triggers the customer's WhatsApp tracking message!

## 💬 3. WhatsApp Automations (Meta Cloud API via Interakt / BiteSpeed)

In India, **60% of fragrance sales are COD**. Unverified COD leads to RTO (Return to Origin) losses of 20-30%. WhatsApp automation brings RTO down below 5%!

### The 4 High-Conversion Automations to Activate:

### 1. COD Anti-RTO Verification Bot
- **Trigger:** Immediate upon Cash on Delivery order placement.
- **Message:**
  > *"Assalamu Alaikum {{Customer_Name}}! You placed an order for {{Product_Title}} (Total ₹{{Total_Amount}}) via Cash on Delivery.*  
  > *Please confirm your order so we can dispatch your perfume package:*  
  > `[ ✅ Confirm Order ]`    `[ ❌ Cancel Order ]`"
- **Result:** Only confirmed orders are packed. Fake/accidental orders are eliminated.

### 2. Abandoned Cart Recovery Bot
- **Trigger:** 20 minutes after user enters phone/email and drops off.
- **Message:**
  > *"Hi {{Customer_Name}}! Your artisanal {{Product_Title}} is waiting in your cart. Stocks are limited from this distillation batch.*  
  > *Complete your purchase now & get 10% OFF + Free Courier with code **ROYAL10**: {{Checkout_URL}}"*
- **Result:** Recovers 20% to 30% of abandoned revenue.

### 3. Live Courier Tracking Push
- **Trigger:** When AWB is generated by Delhivery or Shiprocket.
- **Message:**
  > *"Great news! Your Al-Attar parcel is dispatched via {{Courier_Name}}. Tracking AWB: {{AWB_Number}}.*  
  > *Live Track your package: {{Tracking_URL}}"*

### 4. Post-Delivery Feedback & Re-order Upsell
- **Trigger:** 5 days after successful delivery.
- **Message:**
  > *"How do you like the fragrance notes of {{Product_Title}}? Reply to this message to speak with our fragrance concierge, or use code **ATTAR10** for ₹200 off your next order!"*

## 🚀 5. Quick Steps to Go Live

1. **Connect Shopify Credentials:**
   - In `.env.local`, set:
     ```env
     NEXT_PUBLIC_SHOPIFY_STORE_DOMAIN="your-store.myshopify.com"
     NEXT_PUBLIC_SHOPIFY_STOREFRONT_ACCESS_TOKEN="shpua_your_token"
     ```
2. **Deploy Frontend:**
   - Push this repository to GitHub.
   - Connect repository to **Vercel** (Free or Pro).
   - Point client domain (e.g., `www.clientbrand.com`) to Vercel.
   - Point checkout subdomain (e.g., `checkout.clientbrand.com`) to Shopify.
